🎸 ROCKSTAR

How to play

Each small box starts with 6 cubes. The two big end boxes start empty and are the players’ stores. On your turn, pick any box with cubes (the highlighted ones), lift all its cubes and drop them one by one in the following boxes, in the direction you choose (anticlockwise or clockwise) and including your own store (skip your opponent’s store).

If your last cube lands in a box that already has cubes, lift all of them and keep dropping. When your last cube lands in an empty box or in your store, your turn ends and your opponent plays. When all boxes are empty, count the stores: most cubes wins!
